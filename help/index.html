How support works

  1. Look it up. Most questions are answered in these guides, the troubleshooting table or the FAQ.
  2. Send it from the app. Settings → Send Feedback sends your message with the app version, phone model and calibration date (you can untick those). It works later if you’re offline.
  3. Or email us at info@rimreader.com. We read every message. What to include.
