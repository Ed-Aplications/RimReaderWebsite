The same short animations the app plays before each job, drawn from the same data. The orange part is the part your hand moves. Each lesson plays its steps in turn; tap a step to see it, or Pause.

In the app, find them in Settings → Help. While the tutorial is on, they play by themselves before each calibration and each lap. If your phone or computer is set to reduce motion, they start paused.
