Help

Before you measure

A minute here saves a wasted lap.

Safety first
  • Firm, level ground; engine off; parking brake on; wheels chocked.
  • Never get under, or reach under, a car held up only by a jack. Use rated jack stands. For measuring, the car stays on its wheels: don’t jack it up.
  • Do not adjust worn, bent or damaged parts. Tighten everything to the maker’s torque.
  • Take the phone and the bracket off before the car moves. Never use the app while driving.
  • Test-drive slowly in a safe, empty place, and have a shop check the alignment before highway driving.

The car

  • Ground as level as you can find. Remove unusually heavy cargo.
  • Steering wheel centered: spokes level and the logo upright, the way it sits when you drive straight. Go by the steering wheel, not the tires.
  • Roll forward a few feet in a straight line and stop without touching the brake, so the suspension settles. Then engine off and chock the wheels.
  • Don’t touch the steering wheel again until the lap is done.
  • Set the tire pressures as you normally run them.

The wheels

  • Wheel covers off.
  • Wipe the flat ring at the top of each rim’s lip, where the prongs will sit. Avoid the valve stem and clip-on weights.
  • Never measure off the tire or the hub. The prongs go on the metal lip only.

Windows and doors

  • All windows fully up and doors shut firmly. Nobody in or leaning on the car.
  • If you’ll measure thrust angle, wipe the glass directly above the rubber strip at the back of each front window.

The phone and bracket

  • Let the phone get used to the air for 3 to 5 minutes with the app open, longer going from a warm pocket into the cold or from air conditioning into heat. A phone that is warming up or cooling down drifts, and the lap may not close.
  • Phone clamped in the bracket, screen up. FRONT will point toward the front of the car at every wheel.
  • Calibration current: within 30 days, with this phone and this bracket.
  • Turn up the media volume, so you hear when a reading is taken.

In the app: How to Get Better Readings

The same list appears the first time you start a lap, and any time behind the ? button:

  • Remove unusually heavy cargo
  • On ground as level as you can find
  • Steering wheel centered: spokes level, logo upright
  • Wheel covers off and rim lips wiped
  • Windows fully up and doors shut firmly
  • Nobody in or leaning on the car
