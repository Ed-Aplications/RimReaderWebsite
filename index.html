Rack-free alignment

Align your wheels in your driveway

Rim Reader turns your phone and a 3D-printed bracket into a wheel alignment tool. It reads toe, camber and thrust angle from the phone’s own gyroscope, with no strings, lasers or alignment rack.

How it works

Three steps, one phone

1

Print the bracket

The bracket holds your phone flat and rests two prongs on the rim’s lip. Print ours, or make your own that meets seven simple rules.

2

Calibrate once

Clamp your phone in the bracket and run Bracket Calibration. It measures your bracket’s own small differences, and refuses a phone that moves.

3

Walk the car

Hold the bracket on each wheel when the app asks. It tracks how the phone turns between wheels and shows each wheel’s readings against your vehicle’s spec.

What it measures

The angles that wear your tires

Toe

Front and rear, for each wheel and in total. Toe is the angle you adjust most, and the one that wears tires fastest when it is off.

Camber

The lean of each wheel, with the left-right difference that makes a car pull.

Thrust angle

Where the rear axle points the car. The rear sets the line the front is aligned to.

Caster Coming later

Read by steering the front wheels left and right.

Enter your vehicle’s factory specs, or start from general ranges for everyday cars and SUVs, clearly marked as general. Scan the VIN or tire label with the camera to fill in your vehicle.

Built to be honest

It tells you when it isn’t sure

Checks its own work

It measures its own drift, re-reads a wheel to confirm, and refuses to show a number it cannot stand behind.
