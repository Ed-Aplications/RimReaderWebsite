Hold it on the wheel. The phone does the rest.

Hold the bracket on top of each wheel the screen lights, with its prongs on the rim’s metal lip. When it is level and still, the phone takes the reading by itself and beeps. A full lap of the car takes {{fact:lap-time}}.

What it helps with

The problems an alignment fixes

  • Steering wheel off-centre on a straight road?Rim Reader reads steer-ahead: where your steering wheel will sit when the car drives straight.
  • Tires wearing on one edge?Toe and camber, for each wheel and each axle, checked against your specs.
  • New tie rods, struts or control arms?Check the toe after the work, adjust, and measure again to compare before and after.
  • Car pulling or crabbing down the road?The thrust angle shows whether the rear axle is steering the car, and which rear wheel is off.

For each front wheel it gives one line: turn the front of the tire in or out, and by how much. The angles, in plain words

Three steps

Print, calibrate, walk the car

  1. Print the bracket. Two printed parts, no hardware to buy. Our print files come at launch, or make your own.
  2. Calibrate once a month. A few minutes on a hard floor against a wall. The app reminds you.
  3. Walk the car. Hold the bracket on each wheel in turn. Every result comes with a confidence rating, High, Medium or Low, and the reasons.

What you need

  • An Android phone running {{fact:android}}, with a gyroscope
  • The bracket and one wedge, printed in PETG. No printer? A library, makerspace or print service can print it.
  • Level, firm ground and wheel chocks
  • For calibration: a wall, a strip of tape and a pen

Works offline. No account. Your readings stay on your phone.

Built to be honest

It checks its own work

  • Every wheel is read twice, with a lift between. If the bracket didn’t go back on the same spot, you find out at that wheel.
  • Drift is measured, not guessed. Each wheel is visited twice, so the app measures how far the phone’s gyroscope drifted and removes it.
  • If a lap doesn’t add up, it shows no numbers rather than wrong ones.
