The bracket

A printed bracket that holds your phone true

Two printed parts, the bracket and one wedge, hold your phone square against the rim, the same way every time. No screws, magnets or hardware to buy. It fits phones {{fact:phone-width}} wide in their case.

{{figure:assets/img/bracket/studio-phone.webp|The Rim Reader bracket in graphite, with a phone clamped in it by an orange wedge|The bracket with a phone locked in by its wedge.}}

Will my phone fit?

Find your wedge

One wedge fits each phone width. Measure your phone in its case, at its widest point, and check its left side for buttons (screen toward you, top up).

On a boundary, such as 82.0 mm, either neighbour fits; for a short wedge take the narrower band. If a short wedge presses a button, use the next band down.

A phone whose camera bump starts at its top edge sits slightly nose-up. That is normal: Bracket Calibration measures it and corrects for it.

Fitting the phone, step by step

Getting one

Print ours, have it printed, or make your own

Print ours Files at launch

It prints in PETG on a home printer with a 220 × 220 mm bed, with no supports. The print files will be published when the app launches.

Print settings and care

No printer?

Many libraries, schools and makerspaces have one, and online print services will print a file for you. Ask for PETG.

Make your own

Rim Reader works with a bracket you design yourself if it does seven things, and you run Bracket Calibration with it.

The seven rules

Before every useCheck the bracket for cracks, looseness or wear, and don’t use it if you find any. Keep the prong noses clean: they are the measuring surface.
