Troubleshooting

Find the message you saw and tap it for what it means and what to do.

Check these five first
  1. The phone is pushed into its corner and the wedge is locked.
  2. The prongs are on the rim’s metal lip, not the tire’s rubber.
  3. The rim lip is wiped where the prongs sit.
  4. The phone has settled to the outdoor temperature for {{fact:settle-time}}.
  5. The walks between wheels are short: under 30 seconds each.

During a lap

This needs the bracket calibration for this phone and bracket first…

What it means: No usable calibration yet.

What to do: Run Settings → Bracket Calibration.

Time to calibrate the bracket

What it means: Your calibration is 30 days old or more.

What to do: Calibrate now, or Not now to carry on (it asks again in 30 days).

Place on Ground won’t change to Leave on Ground

What it means: The bracket isn’t still, for example it’s in your hand.

What to do: Set it down and let go.

The bracket moved during the rest. Leave it until prompted.

What it means: It was touched during the ten-second rest.

What to do: Leave it alone until the countdown ends.

Front is too high. Please level. / Back is too high. Please level. / Not level. Measure the top of the rim.

What it means: One prong sits higher than the other.

What to do: Slide the bracket round the rim until the bar sits between the marks.

Amber jittering

What it means: One prong isn’t touching.

What to do: Push in so both noses sit on the lip.

Amber drifting

What it means: The bracket is creeping along the rim.

What to do: Hold still.

Red bumped

What it means: It was knocked.

What to do: Take it off and put it back on.

A low buzz, two quick buzzes

What it means: That reading was refused.

What to do: Hold it again.

Phone Moved? The two readings disagree. Is the phone pushed into its corner? Remove and place on the same spot.

What it means: The second reading at a wheel didn’t match the first: the bracket didn’t go back on the same spot, or the phone shifted in the bracket.

What to do: Push the phone into its corner, lock the wedge, lift the bracket off and place it on the same spot.

Redo Rear / Redo Front The front did not close. Read the front again, starting at…

What it means: That axle’s closing reading failed three times.

What to do: Read only that axle again from the wheel named. The other axle and the doors are kept.

The rear left would not repeat. Read the rear again…

What it means: A wheel’s second reading was refused three times.

What to do: Read that axle again.

Not Closed The lap did not close. Please start over.

What it means: Drift or placement was over the limit after two redos.

What to do: Start over. Work steadily, keep the screen on, let the phone’s temperature settle.

The two readings at one wheel did not agree. Please start over.

What it means: The placement couldn’t be repeated.

What to do: Check the phone is clamped tight, clean the rim lip, start over.

The phone was knocked or spun during a walk. Please start over.

What it means: A drop, knock or spin broke the measurement.

What to do: Carry it smoothly; never set it down mid-lap.

The phone’s temperature was changing during this lap…

What it means: The phone was warming up or cooling down, which makes the readings drift.

What to do: Leave the phone with the app open for 3 to 5 minutes, then start over.

A walk is refused after the screen went off, a call came in, or you switched apps

What it means: The phone stopped reading its sensors mid-walk.

What to do: Keep the screen on and the app in front between wheels.

Readings scatter, with no message

What it means: Often the prongs are touching the tire’s rubber.

What to do: Slide in only until the prong noses meet the lip’s flat ring.

Low confidence

What it means: See the reasons listed under it.

What to do: Fix what it says and measure again before adjusting. Read the result

Doors and camber

Hold it gently against the glass.

What it means: The bracket is wobbling on the door glass.

What to do: Hold it lightly, flat in the back corner.

Hold the bracket flat against the glass.

What it means: A door reading was taken off the glass.

What to do: Lay it flat on the glass.

Thrust angle missing, with a reason

What it means: A door’s two readings disagreed, the bracket was knocked, or more than a minute and a half passed.

What to do: The wheel numbers are still good. Measure again with Thrust if you need it.

Wrong way round: the wedge side is on the rim…

What it means: Camber side reading on the wrong edge.

What to do: Turn the bracket so the wedge side is away from the rim, FRONT still up.

Redo Rear Left (or another tire) during camber

What it means: That tire’s side readings disagreed three times, or it was knocked.

What to do: Read that tire again. If it fails again it is kept as Low.

Camber says relative only

What it means: No camber zero from the calibration.

What to do: Run Bracket Calibration.

The spec check says it needs the floor readings

What it means: The floor readings were skipped for that axle.

What to do: Measure on a flat spot with the floor steps.

Bracket Calibration

The phone moved in the bracket during the flat readings. Push it into the corner, lock the wedge…

What it means: The phone shifted in the tray.

What to do: Re-seat the phone, lock the wedge, start over.

The flat readings could not measure how the phone sits in the bracket

What it means: Not enough good flat readings.

What to do: Start over.

Not flat against the wall. Both prongs must touch the wall.

What it means: One prong is off the wall.

What to do: Slide the bracket flush against the wall.

It did not go back on the same spot. Put it back at your mark… / That turn did not come back to the same spot…

What it means: It was placed away from the V.

What to do: Right prong under the V each time.

Walk canceled due to a drop or knock… / Walk canceled: the sensors stopped… / That walk was too short…

What it means: The walk was spoiled.

What to do: Put it back on the mark and walk again; carry it until it buzzes.

The first and last upright readings disagree. Check that the phone is locked in the bracket, then start over.

What it means: The phone is loose.

What to do: Lock the wedge; start over.

The two edge readings suggest there could be an issue with the bracket. Check the prongs…

What it means: Damaged or dirty prongs.

What to do: Inspect and clean the prongs. Reprint the bracket if one is damaged.

Done, but it cannot be saved.

What it means: The run failed a check; the reason follows.

What to do: Fix the stated cause and run it again.

Scanning, the phone and feedback

This phone has no gyroscope, so it cannot measure alignment.

What it means: The phone has no gyroscope.

What to do: This phone can’t be used.

The VIN scanner refuses a code

What it means: It isn’t a VIN barcode, or the VIN’s check digit doesn’t match.

What to do: Type the VIN, and check the 9th character.

The camera won’t open

What it means: Camera permission was refused.

What to do: Open settings → Permissions → Camera → Allow.

No model after a VIN scan

What it means: No internet connection.

What to do: Pick the model yourself.

Not sent: no internet connection. (Send Feedback)

What it means: You’re offline.

What to do: It’s kept and offered again next time.
