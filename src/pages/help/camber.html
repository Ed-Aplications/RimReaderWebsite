Add camber

Camber is read on a second trip round the car, after the toe lap has closed, so handling the bracket for camber can’t disturb the toe numbers. A camber lap takes a few minutes more.

{{figure:assets/img/render/camber.webp|The bracket standing on its edge against the side of a wheel's rim, FRONT pointing up|The bracket on the side of the rim for camber. In use, you hold it with both hands.}}

Before you start

  • Before Start, tick Camber, with Toe or on its own.
  • Bracket Calibration must be saved: it gives the camber zero. Without it, camber is only relative.
  • Choose the smoothest, flattest floor you can, and wipe away grit beside each tire.

At each tire

With Toe ticked too, do the toe lap as usual. When it closes, the toe card says Camber is next: don’t adjust anything yet. The app then sends you round the car again, starting at the nearest tire.

  1. Place on Ground. Lay the bracket flat on the floor beside the tire, screen up, FRONT forward, and let go until it buzzes.
  2. FRONT Back. Turn it where it lies so FRONT points to the back of the car, and let go again. These two readings measure the floor’s slope.
  3. Measure Top of Rim, as for toe.
  4. Measure Side of Rim. Stand the bracket on its edge against the side of the rim, FRONT pointing at the sky and the wedge side away from the rim. The screen faces the back of the car at the left wheels and the front at the right wheels. Use the same two-hand hold. Pick the front or back of the wheel, whichever is clear of the valve stem, weights and fender.
  5. Place on Same Spot. Lift it off and put it back the same way. The two must agree.

No flat floor beside a tire? Skip floor here leaves that axle uncorrected for the floor. Skip camber here gives up camber at that wheel.

If something goes wrong

  • Wrong way round: the wedge side is on the rim: turn the bracket so the wedge side is away from the rim, FRONT still up.
  • Redo plus a tire’s name, such as Redo Rear Left: that tire’s side readings disagreed three times, or it was knocked. Read it again; if it fails again it is kept, rated Low, and the lap goes on.
  • If the toe lap doesn’t close, no camber is read; start over. Start over during camber keeps the saved toe result.

The result

The Camber card sits under the toe card: each wheel’s camber (negative means the top leans in), each axle’s cross camber (left minus right), a confidence, and what to do.

About the floorThe floor reading measures the patch beside each tire, and the car may sit on a slightly different slope. On one test floor the same spot repeated only to about 0.1°. A flatter spot will match a shop better. Camber is checked against your spec only when it is floor-corrected and calibrated.
