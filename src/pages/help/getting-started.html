Getting started

From a printed bracket to your first result, once through. Each step links to its full guide.

Five steps to your first result

  1. Fit the phone in the bracket. Measure the phone in its case, pick the wedge, phone in against FRONT and the rail, wedge in, push to lock. Wedge and phone
  2. Run Bracket Calibration. Settings → Bracket Calibration. A few minutes at a wall with a strip of tape. Until a calibration is saved, Full Alignment shows only the way to it. Bracket Calibration
  3. Get the car ready. Level ground, steering wheel centred, engine off, wheels chocked, wheel covers off, rim lips wiped. Let the phone settle outside for {{fact:settle-time}}. Before you measure
  4. Walk the car. Tick Toe with Front and Rear, tap Start, answer the steering question, rest the bracket on the ground for ten seconds, then hold it on each wheel the screen lights until it buzzes. Measure toe
  5. Read the result. Check the confidence first, then steer-ahead, thrust, and what to adjust. Read the result

What you need

  • An Android phone running {{fact:android}}, with a gyroscope. If yours has none, the app says so. The iPhone version is coming later.
  • A bracket and one wedge, printed in PETG. Our print files will be published when the app launches, or you can make your own.
  • For calibration: a hard, flat floor beside a wall or bookshelf, a strip of tape and a pen.
  • For measuring: level, firm ground, wheel chocks, and {{fact:lap-time}} per toe lap.

The first time the app opens, it shows the Terms of Use and Safety: tick the box confirming you are 18 or older and agree, and tap Accept. While the tutorial is on, short animated lessons play before each calibration and each lap.

{{more:A tour of the app|tour}}

The app opens on Full Alignment. At the top, Select Vehicle opens your garage. Above the car drawing is the Measure menu: Toe, Camber, Thrust (and Caster, greyed out until it is built), with Front and Rear underneath. Start begins a lap. The ? button opens How to Get Better Readings, and the slider icon at the top right opens Settings:
