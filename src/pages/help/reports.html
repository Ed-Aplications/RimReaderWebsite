History, reports and sharing

Every lap is saved on your phone. Compare two of them, share a picture of a result, or make a three-page PDF report to print or send.

History and compare

  • Settings → Garage & History: the clock at the top lists every lap and camber run, newest first. A vehicle’s own clock lists only its readings.
  • Tap a reading to see the car drawing with every number, the advice, the confidence and the spec check.
  • Compare: tick two runs and tap Compare. Each shared number shows before → after with the change, or no measurable change when the difference is within the app’s repeatability.
  • Note: the note button takes up to 280 characters, such as what you adjusted.

Share a picture

On a finished, saved lap, or a reading opened from History, tap Share. It sends a picture of the car drawing and the result card through your phone’s share sheet: email, messages, Drive. Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

Make a PDF report

Tap PDF. The app makes an Alignment Report, saves it in your phone’s Download/Rim Reader folder and opens it, ready to share or print. The Files app finds it again later. Paper is US Letter in the US, Canada, Mexico and the Philippines, and A4 elsewhere.

  1. Page 1 is laid out like a shop’s printout: vehicle, tire size, VIN and date; what the numbers were checked against; every number with its spec range, a small bar and its status in words; the confidence; and notes on the steering wheel, tie rods and rear wheels.
  2. Page 2, “Your Alignment at a Glance”: counts of In spec, Near the limit and Out of spec; the car from above with each tire coloured and turned; thrust and steer-ahead arrows; and how the steering wheel will sit.
  3. Page 3, “Understanding Your Alignment”: picture cards for toe, camber, caster, thrust angle, steer-ahead and cross camber, and more terms.

Every page ends with the estimate notice. Your own notes aren’t printed on a personal report; the VIN is.
