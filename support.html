Support

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: email info@middletnautorepair.com. We read every message.

To help us help you

  • Your phone’s make and model.
  • The app version (Settings, About).
  • What you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps.

You can also send feedback from inside the app: Settings, Send Feedback. The privacy policy lists exactly what it sends.

Your data

Rim Reader has no accounts, and your measurements, vehicles and settings stay on your phone. To delete them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone’s settings. If you sent us feedback and want it deleted, email us and we will delete it.

Safety

Rim Reader gives estimates. Work on firm, level ground with the engine off, the parking brake on and the wheels chocked, and have a qualified shop check your alignment before highway driving. The full rules are in the Terms of Use and Safety.
